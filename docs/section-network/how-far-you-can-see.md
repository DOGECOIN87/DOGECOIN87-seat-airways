# Your seat is how far you can see

The **Section network** is a directory for holders: each can publish a card, introduce themselves, and talk in their cabin's room. What your seat decides is **how far you can see** — and the rule is one line, applied everywhere:

{% hint style="success" %}
**You can see down the aircraft, never up.** Your seat opens your own section and every seated section behind it. The sections ahead of you keep their contact details and their conversations to themselves.
{% endhint %}

## Who can read what

| | Who can read it |
| --- | --- |
| **A holder's name and role** | Every holder signed in to the directory. The roster belongs to the whole cabin. |
| **A holder's contact details** — email, website, LinkedIn | Their own section, and every section **ahead** of theirs. Never a section behind them. |
| **A conversation between two holders** | The two wallets on it, plus any section seated **ahead of both** of them. |
| **A cabin's room** | That cabin, and every cabin ahead of it. Only that cabin can post in it. |
| **The PA** | Everyone aboard, the cargo hold included. |

Put the other way round — what **your** cabin opens to you:

| Your cabin | Contact details you can read | Conversations you can overhear | Rooms you hear |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Flight Deck** | Everyone seated | Any two holders in First, Business, Exit Row or Economy | All five |
| **First** | First, Business, Exit Row, Economy | Any two holders in Business, Exit Row or Economy | First, Business, Exit Row, Economy |
| **Business** | Business, Exit Row, Economy | Any two holders in Exit Row or Economy | Business, Exit Row, Economy |
| **Exit Row** | Exit Row, Economy | Any two holders in Economy | Exit Row, Economy |
| **Economy** | Economy | None | Economy |
| **Cargo hold** | None | None | None — but you hear the PA |

## Three consequences, on purpose

* **The flight deck hears the whole aircraft, and Economy — with no cabin behind it — overhears nothing.**
* **No section can read its own peers' private conversations.** A conversation with one end level with you is not behind you, so First Class reads every conversation in Business and Economy, and none of the other First Class ones.
* **The cargo hold is not a cabin.** A wallet without a seat is on no roster: its card is not shown and its conversations are not read out, to anyone.

## Writing goes exactly as far as reading

If you can read somebody's card, you can introduce yourself to them — and nothing carries forward. So the flight deck can write to anybody on the aircraft, and only the flight deck can write to the flight deck. The further forward you sit, the fewer people can reach you: the view forward is what the next seat up buys, and so is the quiet.

{% hint style="info" %}
These rules are enforced by the server, not just by the page. A card you are not entitled to arrives without its contact details, and a message to a cabin ahead of yours is refused: _"That cabin is ahead of yours. Introductions carry aft, never forward."_
{% endhint %}
