# Altitude bands

Because market cap _is_ altitude, the milestones are places. Crossing one changes the whole world outside the windows, and the PA says so.

| Market cap | Band | Outside | On the PA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| under $1M | **In the weather** | Terrain, cloud and haze below you | _"Back in the weather. Seat belt sign is on."_ — when you drop back into it |
| $1M | **Above the clouds** | You break out on top of the cloud deck; the air above thins and deepens | _"We are on top. Cloud deck below us."_ |
| $10M | **Space** | The sky drains from the top down, the ground becomes a curved horizon, and the stars come out | _"Cabin crew, the sky has run out. Sky is black."_ |
| $50M | **The moon** | The lunar surface under a low sun, with Earthrise off the left side | _"Ladies and gentlemen, we have reached the moon."_ |

Above $10M the horizon keeps bending the higher the market cap goes, so the climb is visible all the way to the moon.

## Where you are right now

* The label at the top of the page — **Live · SA350 · In the weather**, for example — names the current band.
* The **climb meter** under the view shows the current band at one end and the next at the other, with how far through the climb you are. It is measured on a log scale, so going from $1M to $2M moves it as much as going from $5M to $10M.

{% hint style="info" %}
The bands are about altitude only. The sky's colour and weather within the band come from your own clock and local weather — see [The sky outside](the-sky.md).
{% endhint %}
