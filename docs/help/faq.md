# FAQ

## Seats

<details>

<summary>Can I choose my seat?</summary>

No. Every holder is ranked by balance and seated in rank order, so your seat is whatever your holding earns against everybody else's. You can _look_ from any seat you like — see [The seat ladder](../the-cabin/seat-ladder.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>Why am I in the cargo hold?</summary>

Either you are ranked below the 178th seat, or your balance has not been read yet — the check-in card says **unread** under Holding when that happens. The Boarding ladder shows what the last seat aboard is holding.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Why did my seat change?</summary>

Somebody's balance changed — yours, or a holder's near you. Out-hold the holder ahead of you and you take their seat; be out-held and you move back. The PA announces your new seat.

</details>

<details>

<summary>I just bought more. How long until my seat updates?</summary>

Your own balance is re-read every 2 minutes, and straight away when you check in or reload the page. The rest of the holder list is refreshed every 90 seconds.

</details>

<details>

<summary>What is "Souls on board"?</summary>

The number of holders seated on the aircraft, up to 178. How many ride below the cutoff is on the placard in the cargo hold.

</details>

## Costs and safety

<details>

<summary>Does any of this cost anything?</summary>

No. Checking in, putting up an advert, publishing a card and sending messages are free. Seat Airlines never asks your wallet for a transaction — only plain-text signatures, which cost nothing. See [Wallet safety](../safety/wallet-safety.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>Which wallets work? Can I use my phone?</summary>

Phantom, Solflare and Backpack. On a phone, open seat-airlines.space in your wallet app's built-in browser to check in. See [Connecting a wallet](../getting-started/connecting-a-wallet.md).

</details>

## The flight

<details>

<summary>Why does it say 163,000 ft for a moment when the page opens?</summary>

That is the starting value shown until the first market reading arrives, usually within a second or two. If the market cannot be reached, the instruments hold their last reading.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Why is it night outside?</summary>

The sky follows **your** clock. If it is night where you are, it is night outside the aircraft. See [The sky outside](../how-it-flies/the-sky.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>Why does the Outside reading say "Modelled weather"?</summary>

Live weather could not be fetched — you may be offline, or a blocker stopped the request — so a modelled sky stands in.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Why is the aircraft rolling or upside down?</summary>

The flight crew can take the controls for show, and everyone sees it at once. The altitude and attitude still come from the market. See [One number flies the plane](../how-it-flies/flight-model.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>I have never seen the Brace lamp lit. Is it broken?</summary>

No. Brace lights past 23° nose-down, and the steepest dive the market can produce is about 18°. See [The overhead panel and the PA](../how-it-flies/overhead-panel.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>Why does the board at the top keep changing?</summary>

It is a split-flap departure board, and it turns through the airline's lines: _Hold more, fly higher_, _Take a seat_, _Network_, _Build_, _Relax_ and more. If your device is set to reduce motion, it shows _Hold more, fly higher_ and stays still.

</details>

## Adverts

<details>

<summary>Other people can't see my advert.</summary>

If the PA said it went up _in this browser only_, the advert server could not be reached and your advert was saved on your device instead. Publish it again later. See [Put an advert on your seat](../the-wall/put-an-advert-on-your-seat.md#when-something-goes-wrong).

</details>

<details>

<summary>How do I remove or change my advert?</summary>

Publish a new one to replace it. A published advert also comes down on its own if your wallet leaves the manifest.

</details>

## Section network

<details>

<summary>Why can't I see somebody's email or links?</summary>

They are seated ahead of you. Contact details are readable by the holder's own section and the sections ahead of theirs, never the ones behind. See [Your seat is how far you can see](../section-network/how-far-you-can-see.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>Why can't I send somebody an introduction?</summary>

You can only write to holders whose card you can read — your own section and every section behind it. Introductions never carry forward, and there is a limit of 20 messages an hour.

</details>
