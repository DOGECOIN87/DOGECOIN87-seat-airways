# Cabins and seats

## The cabins

| Cabin | Ranks | Rows | Layout | Seats | Boarding group |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Flight Deck** | 1–2 | — | Captain (**CPT**) and First Officer (**FO**) | 2 | 1 |
| **First** | 3–10 | 1–2 | 2-2 — seats A B · E F | 8 | 1 |
| **Business** | 11–40 | 3–7 | 3-3 — seats A B C · D E F | 30 | 2 |
| **Exit Row** | 41–52 | 16–17 | 3-3 | 12 | 3 |
| **Economy** | 53–178 | 8–15 and 18–30 | 3-3 | 126 | 4 |
| **Cargo hold** | 179 and below | — | below the floor | unlimited | — |

In a 3-3 row, **A** and **F** are windows, **C** and **D** are aisles, and **B** and **E** are middles. First is 2-2, so its **B** and **E** are aisle seats and it has no middles.

## What your seat changes

These are real differences, not decoration:

* **Your square on the wall.** The wall is laid out like the cabin, front at the top, and front seats are drawn bigger: the flight deck at 1.5× and First at 1.22× the size of the rest. See [Every seat is a billboard](../the-wall/every-seat-is-a-billboard.md).
* **Your view.** A window seat has the window beside it. An aisle seat looks down the aisle with the window a seat away, and a middle seat has a neighbour's shoulder between it and the daylight. See [Views and controls](views-and-controls.md).
* **How far you can see in the directory.** Your own section and every cabin behind it. See [Your seat is how far you can see](../section-network/how-far-you-can-see.md).
* **The PA.** The flight deck can make one announcement a day that the whole aircraft hears. See [Introductions, rooms and the PA](../section-network/introductions-rooms-and-the-pa.md).

## What your boarding pass says

Each cabin prints its own line on your [boarding pass](boarding-pass.md). Apart from the flight deck's PA, these are in-flight humour rather than features:

| Cabin | Printed on the pass |
| --- | --- |
| Flight Deck | _You have the PA. One announcement a day. Use it well._ |
| First | _Lie-flat. Champagne on every green candle._ |
| Business | _Priority boarding, and first off the aircraft in an emergency landing._ |
| Exit Row | _You have agreed to open that door. Sign the message._ |
| Economy | _Seat back and tray table. Welcome aboard._ |
| 30B and 30E | _Middle seat, last row, by the lavatory. Does not recline. Merch this._ |
