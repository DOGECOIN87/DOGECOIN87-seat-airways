# The seat ladder

## The rules

1. Every wallet holding the token is ranked by balance, biggest first.
2. Rank 1 gets the best seat on the aircraft, rank 2 the next best, and so on down all **178 seats**.
3. Everyone ranked below 178 rides in the **cargo hold**.
4. The ranking is live. Out-hold the holder directly ahead of you and you take their seat; they move back one.

You cannot choose a seat, swap one, or reserve one. Your bag is your seat.

{% hint style="info" %}
Only people are seated. Accounts owned by a program — a pump.fun bonding curve, a liquidity pool — hold tokens but are not passengers, so they are left out of the ranking.
{% endhint %}

## Which seat is better

Seats are ordered the way the aircraft is laid out:

1. **By cabin**, front to back: Flight Deck, First, Business, Exit Row, Economy.
2. **By row**, front to back, inside each cabin.
3. **Inside a row:** window first, then aisle, then middle.

| Ranks | Cabin | Seats |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–2 | **Flight Deck** | CPT (rank 1), FO (rank 2) |
| 3–10 | **First** | rows 1–2 |
| 11–40 | **Business** | rows 3–7 |
| 41–52 | **Exit Row** | rows 16–17 |
| 53–178 | **Economy** | rows 8–15 and 18–30 |
| 179 and below | **Cargo hold** | everyone else |

For example: rank 3 is **1A**, rank 4 is **1F** (the two First windows in row 1), rank 5 is **1B** and rank 6 is **1E** (its aisles). Rank 11 is **3A**, the first Business window. The last two seats aboard, ranks 177 and 178, are **30B** and **30E** — the middle seats in the last row, by the lavatory.

{% hint style="warning" %}
The Exit Row is its own cabin and ranks ahead of **all** of Economy — including Economy rows 8–15, which physically sit in front of it. Rank 52 is 17E in the Exit Row; rank 53 is 8A.
{% endhint %}

See [Cabins and seats](cabins-and-seats.md) for each cabin's layout.

## Moving up

The **Boarding ladder**, in the Check in section, lists every cabin with the ranks it holds and marks yours. Under it, **Next up** names the holder directly ahead of you — their rank, their seat and their balance — and how many more tokens it takes to reach them.

When your seat changes, the camera walks you to it and the PA announces it: _"Passenger reseated to 7C. BUSINESS."_

## How often it updates

* The holder list is re-read every **90 seconds**, and the server behind it keeps the chain's answer for a minute.
* Your own balance is re-read every **2 minutes**, and it counts the moment you check in — you do not wait for the next refresh.
* Updates pause while the tab is in the background and resume when you come back.

## The cargo hold

Everyone below the cutoff rides below the floor. It is not a punishment: unpressurised, and by far the biggest room on the aircraft, it is where most holders live. Walk down to it with **Cargo hold** under the view; its placard counts how many holders are riding below the cutoff.

If you are in the hold, the Boarding ladder tells you what the last seat aboard is holding. While fewer than 178 wallets hold the token, some seats stay unsold, and any balance at all takes one.

## Looking is free

You can walk the camera to any seat on the aircraft — click it on the wall, or use **Walk the aircraft** — and see exactly what it sees. Only your holding decides where you are ticketed.
