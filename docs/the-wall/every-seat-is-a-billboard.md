# Every seat is a billboard

Every seat on the map is a square, so every held seat is a billboard. Its holder can put a square image on it, and the aircraft reads as a mosaic with the best-placed adverts at the front. Placement is earned by holding, never bought: **row 1 is not for sale, only for holding.**

## Reading the wall

The wall is the seat map in **The wall** section, laid out like the aircraft with the front at the top.

* **An open seat** is a socket pressed into the fuselage — nobody holds it.
* **A held seat** is a tile raised out of it.
* **Your seat** is the one lit blue.
* Front seats are drawn bigger — the flight deck at 1.5× and First at 1.22× — because rank 1 and rank 178 are not the same placement.
* Runs of rows nobody holds are folded into a single strip; **Show** unfolds them.

Select any seat to read it in the panel beside the map: the seat and its rank, the holder's address, their bag and share of supply, and the advert with its description and link. Selecting a seat also puts the camera in it.

## House adverts

A held seat whose holder has not put anything up carries Seat Airlines' own campaigns — the way unsold inventory does on a real aircraft. Each one says in its description that the seat's holder can replace it.

## Adverts follow the wallet

An advert is stored against the **wallet** that published it, not against a seat. So:

* Get out-held and reseated from **3A** to **7C**, and your advert moves with you.
* Drop off the manifest altogether, and it comes down on its own.

## Where adverts appear

* **On the wall**, at the position the holder has earned.
* **In the cabin.** Take a seat and look forward: the seat-back screens in the row ahead show those seats' adverts.
