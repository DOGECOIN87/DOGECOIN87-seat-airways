# Quick start

### 1. Open the site

Go to [seat-airlines.space](https://seat-airlines.space). The page opens outside, on the whole aircraft, flying at the altitude its market cap puts it. The split-flap board at the top turns through the airline's lines — _Hold more, fly higher_ first.

### 2. Get the token

The contract address is on the strip across the very top of the page, marked **CA**. **Copy** copies it exactly, and the **pump.fun** button opens the token's page. See [The token](the-token.md) for the address and how to check it.

### 3. Check in with a wallet

Scroll to **Check in** at the bottom of the page and press **Check in with a wallet**. Phantom, Solflare and Backpack all work. Connecting shares your public address and nothing else — see [Connecting a wallet](connecting-a-wallet.md).

### 4. Take your seat

You do not pick a seat: your holding does. If you are among the 178 biggest holders, the camera walks you to your seat, the PA announces it, and your boarding pass fills in. If you are not, you ride in the cargo hold, and the **Boarding ladder** under the check-in card tells you what the last seat aboard is holding.

Once you are seated, the Boarding ladder shows who is directly ahead of you and how many more tokens it takes to pass them.

## Then

* **Look around.** Turn your head, walk the aircraft, visit the flight deck — [Views and controls](../the-cabin/views-and-controls.md).
* **Put an image on your seat.** Find your seat on the wall and press **Advertise here** — [Put an advert on your seat](../the-wall/put-an-advert-on-your-seat.md).
* **Publish a networking card.** Sign in to the directory once and publish — [Cards and sign-in](../section-network/cards-and-sign-in.md).

{% hint style="success" %}
Checking in, advertising and networking cost nothing. The only things your wallet is ever asked for are plain-text signatures — see [Wallet safety](../safety/wallet-safety.md).
{% endhint %}
